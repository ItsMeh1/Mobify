FAQ

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan remains active until the end of the billing cycle.

Do yearly plans save money?

Yearly billing includes a 20% discount compared to monthly pricing.

What is Supporter?

Supporter helps fund development and hosting, and unlocks community perks and early access.

When I upgrade, where do I recive the perks>

Your Flash & Mobify accounts are linked. Using your same account, you can login to both services, and you will recive perks on both platforms.

Can I share my account so others will get the perks?>

Sharing accounts are not tollerated, and will lead to a permanent ban.

If you suspect your account has been compromised, ask for help immediately on the Discord server, or on Mobify if it is quicker there.
